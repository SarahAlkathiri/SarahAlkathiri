## Projects

### 🏨 What do hotel guests in Makkah & Madinah complain about?
2,100 Arabic reviews. A small AI model reads each review's sentiment (~94% accuracy), then all the analysis is in Excel: complaint topics, a hotel × topic heatmap, and "hidden complaints" where guests gave high stars but wrote negative text.
**Excel · Python · Sentiment analysis** → [Project](https://github.com/SarahAlkathiri/sarahalkathiri.github.io/tree/main/projects/hotel-reviews-ai)

### 🚨 Who's about to quit? Attrition early warning in Excel
Every employee gets a 0–100 risk score from seven signals (time since last raise, pay vs peers, satisfaction, performance drop, overtime, time since promotion, career stage), with the main reason and a suggested action. Includes a manager dashboard and an interactive employee card.
**Excel** → [Project](https://github.com/SarahAlkathiri/sarahalkathiri.github.io/tree/main/projects/hr-attrition-early-warning)

### 💼 HR & finance system in Excel
One connected workbook for Saudi businesses: payroll with GOSI (old and new system), end-of-service calculator (Articles 84 & 85), leave balances, 15% VAT invoices and returns, budget vs actual, and a dashboard.
**Excel** → [Project](https://github.com/SarahAlkathiri/sarahalkathiri.github.io/tree/main/projects/saudi-hr-finance-system)

### 🔑 Saudi rents 2019–2024
An Excel dashboard on Ejar rental contracts across all 13 regions. I combined 13 messy files with Power Query and used weighted averages. Riyadh apartment rents went up 38%.
**Excel · Power Query** → [Project](https://github.com/SarahAlkathiri/sarahalkathiri.github.io/tree/main/projects/saudi-rental-dashboard)

### 📺 stc tv viewer behaviour
About 1M viewing sessions. Series fans are 14% of users but 69% of watch hours, and most of them watch in SD because the series catalogue itself is mostly SD.
**Python · Pandas** → [Project](https://github.com/SarahAlkathiri/sarahalkathiri.github.io/tree/main/projects/stc-tv-analysis)

### 🏥 Aspect-based sentiment analysis of patient reviews
My MSc project at Universiti Malaya. I compared classical ML, LSTMs and transformers on 82K patient reviews; BERT reached an F1 of 0.93.
**Python · BERT · RoBERTa · DistilBERT**
